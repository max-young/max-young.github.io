What I work on

At Rino.ai, I own architecture, product definition, and technical direction for a vehicle-platform team. I have built reusable delivery infrastructure for R&D, factory, and fleet operations—including a distributed FOTA MVP for TBOX/ADCU nodes, an end-to-end vehicle calibration platform, and fleet incident and route-delivery tools. The work spans failure recovery, power-loss safety, large-map distribution, resumable transfers, deployment automation, service health, and version/state consistency across thousands of vehicles.

Earlier, I built backend and full-stack systems for online education, e-commerce, and B2B SaaS at Dedao and Kaoshixing. My background began in geomatics and map quality, which continues to inform my work on vehicle data, mapping, calibration, and field operations.
